About Edfan College

Like a mustard seed, a small beginning with a great purpose.

Established in 2008 and fully open since 2012, Edfan College in Ikorodu, Lagos raises young people with sharp minds, strong values and the confidence to stand among the best in the world.

Founder’s message

Raising a generation of patriotic, value-driven leaders.

Edfan College was born out of a burning passion to provide quality, self-reliant secondary education for children in Nigeria. We believe in raising patriotic citizens with the virtues and values to place our nation alongside the most respected in the world. That is why Edfan sits in a calm, secure environment, free from everyday distractions. Because surroundings shape the mind, attitude and growth of a child, we have invested in beautiful infrastructure and facilities that inspire learning. Our founders are successful professionals who carefully appoint teachers and staff who are competent, efficient and committed. Ours is a learner-centred college: every day, our staff show kindness, empathy and care to the students in their charge. Our trained counsellors guide each child in subject choices, relationships and building healthy self-esteem. Led by an experienced and devoted educationist, our team looks beyond exam success. We nurture total persons, prepared for a brighter future and academic excellence. To every parent who has trusted us with their child: thank you. Edfan College remains a testimony of excellence, true to our slogan, “A Brighter Future Assured!” Mr Abade Endurance Chukwudi, Founder
