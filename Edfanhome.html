Welcome to Edfan

More than passing exams. We grow total persons.

Founded on a passion for quality, self-reliant education, Edfan College is a co-educational day school set in a serene, secure part of Ikorodu. We blend the National and Lagos State curricula with global best practice, so your child gains strong grades and the values to lead.

  • ✓Non-discriminatory admission: we believe every child can excel.
  • ✓Individual learning paths tailored to each student.
  • ✓Counsellors guiding subject choices and self-esteem.

Why parents choose us

Four reasons families trust Edfan with their children.

Every child is seen

With around 18 students per class and an advisor system, no child is left behind. Each student follows an individual learning path.

Results that open doors

Our WAEC, NECO and BECE results are consistently strong, and our alumni excel in universities across Nigeria and abroad.

Learning by doing

Modern Physics, Chemistry and Biology labs, plus workshops for technology, textiles, food and fine art, turn theory into real skill.

Safe, caring and disciplined

A secure, serene school premises, an on-site clinic, trained counsellors and a culture of self-discipline and respect.

Academics

A curriculum built for today, and for what comes next.

In Junior School (JS1–JS3), students explore 15 subject areas from Mathematics and Sciences to French, Yoruba, ICT and Art. In Senior School (SS1–SS3), after career consultation, they choose from Sciences, Humanities, Business, Technology and the Arts, working towards WAEC and NECO.

Lessons blend face-to-face teaching, technology and peer collaboration, so learning goes deeper.
